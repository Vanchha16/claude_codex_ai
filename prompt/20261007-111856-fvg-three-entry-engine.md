## Proposed baseline rules (declare before observing results)

These are predeclared engineering starting settings for this approved build, not claimed profitable rules or separately user-selected trading parameters. Implement one predeclared v1 rather than a parameter search.
1. Closed contiguous M5 Bid candles; complete valid M15 groups only. FVG uses adjacent closed M15 candles A/B/C. BUY zone [A.high,C.low] if C.low>A.high; SELL zone [C.high,A.low] if C.high<A.low. Zone only becomes available at C close; C's component M5 bars cannot retest it.
2. Qualification: gap >= max(2 ticks, 0.10 * M15 ATR14); middle candle B directional body >= 1.0 * ATR14. Wilder ATR SMA seeded from 14 true ranges, computed through B only. EMA20>EMA50 permits BUY, reverse permits SELL, equal neither; SMA-seeded over the closed contiguous M15 run ending at C, at least 50 bars. A market/data break resets warmup; report this limitation truthfully.
3. Retest/confirmation: first subsequent closed M5 bar that intersects the FVG zone is the retest. Freeze its high for BUY / low for SELL. A different one of the next three contiguous closed M5 candles must close strictly beyond that level in the continuation direction. Invalidation takes precedence: close beyond the zone's far edge; missing/invalid candles. First touch cannot confirm itself. Zone/setup lifetime two hours; no stale historical setup submission after startup/restart/resume/reconnect.
4. After confirmation, plan three resting BUY LIMIT or SELL LIMIT entries at 1%,50%,80% depth. BUY depth is measured down from zone top; SELL up from zone bottom. Round BUY entries downward, SELL upward to current broker tick. Reject zones too narrow for three distinct valid entry prices. Do not convert an already-crossed level into a market order, move requested depths, or silently place fewer than three legs due to preflight failure.
5. SL: one common stop beyond the zone far edge with a two-tick buffer, rounded outward. Each TP = its own entry +/- 2 * abs(entry-SL), rounded outward. No trailing/breakeven in v1. Broker stops/volume/expiry/margin constraints must pass without hidden widening or increased risk; otherwise reject the whole plan before submission.
6. Risk: **fixed $10 USD total per setup**, split into equal nominal $10/3 per-leg allocations. Make the combined cash amount and its current equity percentage visible. The same $10 budget applies as equity changes unless the user explicitly changes it; do not silently convert it to 5% equity risk. Validate account currency/units or use an explicitly supported conversion; never silently treat cent-account units or other currencies as USD. Volume is calculated using order_calc_profit in account currency and rounded DOWN to broker lot step. Never raise to minimum lots if it exceeds allocation; reject the whole plan if any leg cannot fit. Do not redistribute unfilled-leg risk. Report fees/gap/slippage limitations; nominal SL risk is not a hard loss cap. Do not auto-arm on entry or persistence of the confirmed risk value. Explicit execution opt-in must bind source, symbol, account and strategy version, separately from Telegram opt-in; invalidate on account/source/symbol changes.
7. Capacity: one open FVG basket per symbol (up to three legs), no competing symbol exposure; 30-minute cooldown and max four newly accepted baskets per Bangkok date (12 planned legs maximum). State basket count and leg count separately; a cap is not a quota. Switching/restart must not reset accepted-basket history. Unfilled pending legs expire two hours after placement using supported broker expiry. Open positions remain protected by their SL/TP; do not silently impose an additional forced-market-close rule. Cancel only this engine's remaining pending legs when the basket/FVG invalidates, following supported broker constraints; never touch unrelated orders/positions.
8. Live lifecycle: durable reservation before every send, broker preflight all three, truthful per-leg states, symbol/account magic/comment/ticket ownership, pending/fill/cancel/expire/partial/unknown handling, reconciliation against broker orders/positions/history/deals after restart. Unknown sends cannot be resent blindly; partial batches cannot be described as fully submitted. Preserve protective SL/TP on accepted orders. Netting accounts must fail clearly rather than merge away the independent targets; hedging supported.
9. Alerts: use the existing explicitly enabled Telegram/outbox path for the planned three entry levels, with Entry/TP/SL/RR data, separate clear ladder/order statuses in the dashboard.
