### 2. Define CRT-SMC-v1 exactly

Use the following initial configurable definitions. Document them as our version, not an assertion of one universal ICT/SMC/CRT standard:

A. Reference range: A is a completed H1 candle. B is the immediately following H1 candle, also completed and contiguous. At B close, evaluate A/B once. Require positive A range and sufficient preceding M5 history.

B. Sweep/reclaim:
- BUY candidate: B.low <= A.low - 2*tick_size, B.high <= A.high, and A.low < B.close < A.high.
- SELL candidate: B.high >= A.high + 2*tick_size, B.low >= A.low, and A.low < B.close < A.high.
- Reject double-sided sweeps, closes on/outside A boundaries, noncontiguous hours, and invalid prices. A two-tick minimum sweep is a configurable baseline.

C. Structure level: define a swing high/low using two CLOSED M5 bars on each side and a strict extremum (ties are not pivots). Its availability time is the close of the second right-hand bar. For BUY freeze the most recent confirmed swing high; for SELL freeze the most recent confirmed swing low. Select only pivots confirmed at or before B OPEN, from the preceding 24 M5 bars, with their level strictly inside A range. If none exists, reject the candidate. Never relabel a past signal when later bars create pivots.

D. Confirmation: after B CLOSE, wait up to 12 subsequent completed M5 bars (one hour) for a close crossing the frozen structure level in the candidate direction. BUY: previous M5 close <= level and current close > level; SELL: previous close >= level and current close < level. The first eligible confirmation bar opens at/after B close. Reject a confirmation closing outside A range. Before confirmation, cancel if the sweep extreme is revisited/broken, the opposite range boundary is touched, continuity is lost, or the deadline passes. On a bar/tick containing both invalidation and confirmation, invalidation takes precedence. Check expiry and invalidation every scan, not just at hour changes. Consume each candidate at most once.

E. Entry and levels: at confirmation obtain the first fresh executable quote, BUY at Ask, SELL at Bid. SL is beyond B's sweep extreme by two tick sizes, rounded outward to the instrument's tick grid. TP is the opposite edge of A's range. Require SL < entry < TP for BUY or TP < entry < SL for SELL. Require reward/risk >= 1.5 after bid/ask entry and max spread <= 0.50 in XAUUSD price units (USD per ounce), not points/pips. All thresholds are editable validated config; 1.5 and 0.50 are testing defaults. Do not assume that these defaults are suitable for every Exness contract. Record quote time, actual spread, symbol metadata and config version.

F. Timing/freshness: an actionable live confirmation is eligible for at most 30 seconds after its candle close. Quotes older than 30 seconds, invalid spreads, terminal disconnects or missed confirmations are not actionable. If an entry check fails, consume/reject the candidate with a reason rather than generating a delayed entry later. Do not send historical signals after startup/reconnection. Warm up from history and record a live-start watermark before enabling delivery.

G. Lifecycle: record candidates, invalidations, expirations, rejections and confirmed signals with reason fields. One confirmed signal per symbol/A/B/config version; deduplicate persistently across scans and process restarts. Prevent overlapping active BUY/SELL simulated signals for the same symbol. A pause stops new alerts; the outcome tracker may continue recording existing simulated signals.
