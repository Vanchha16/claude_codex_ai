About zones

FVG (Fair Value Gap): three candles where the 3rd candle’s low is at least one tick above the 1st candle’s high (bullish), or its high at least one tick below the 1st candle’s low (bearish). Drawn from the 3rd candle.

IFVG (Inverse Fair Value Gap): an FVG that a candle closed through on the other side; it flips direction from that candle on.

OB (Order Block): when a candle closes beyond the latest confirmed swing high/low (2 candles on each side), the last opposite-colour candle before it (up to 20 back) becomes the zone. A price-structure convention, not proof of institutional orders.

BB (Breaker Block): an order block that a candle closed through; it flips direction from that candle on.

Colours show the type: FVG IFVG OB BB; the badge arrow shows direction (▲ bullish, ▼ bearish).

Zones end only on a candle close beyond them (wicks don’t count). They are built from closed candles of the current timeframe and the history loaded on the chart. By default the newest 3 active zones of each type are drawn; choose 3/5/10, or turn on History to also show recently ended zones (faded). “FVG 3/14” means 3 drawn of 14 active. Not used by CRT-SMC-v1 signals or Telegram.
